Flagship

Judge Audit — the Moody's for AI judges

AI judges (LLM-as-a-judge, probabilistic decision APIs) now decide what gets shipped, filtered and approved. Judge Audit is the independent harness that audits their calibration: when a judge says "90% confident", is it right 90% of the time? We compute ECE, reliability diagrams, accuracy–coverage curves, cost and latency — and ship signed, reproducible evidence dossiers aligned with the EU AI Act.

Measure

Calibration, not vibes

Expected Calibration Error, reliability diagrams, zero-error coverage: the statistics that say whether a judge's confidence means anything at all.

Reproduce

Every number, re-runnable

Raw checkpoints live in the repo. Anyone can re-run an audit and get the same numbers. No black-box claims — including about ourselves.

Prove

Evidence for the AI Act

Signed dossiers designed to support high-risk AI obligations (Arts. 12, 14, 15): logging, human oversight, accuracy and robustness.
