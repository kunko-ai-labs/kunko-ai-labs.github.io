Privacy and security

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What this site is

kunkoai.com is a static website. It has no forms, no accounts, no login and no database of our own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare.

What our hosting providers process

To deliver the pages and keep them secure, GitHub and Cloudflare process visitors' IP addresses and basic request data. Their handling is described in their own notices:

  • GitHub General Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

These providers may also set technical cookies of their own as part of delivery or security. We do not control them.

What is stored in your browser

The only thing the site stores is one entry in your browser's localStorage, named kunko-theme, which remembers whether you chose the light or the dark theme. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. We set no cookies, and the site uses no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds.

Who runs this site

This site is operated by the Kunko AI Labs project. For anything about your personal data, write to info@kunkoai.com.

Contact

If you write to info@kunkoai.com, we receive your email address and whatever you send us, and we use it only to answer you.

Your rights

Under data protection law (GDPR) you can ask us, in plain terms, to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you (access);
  • correct it if it is wrong (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure);
  • stop using it (objection).

Write to info@kunkoai.com and we will answer. If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to your national data protection authority. In Spain that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

Pilot data

Pilot data is never sent through this website. The terms (where it runs, retention, confidentiality, NDA/DPA) are agreed in writing before any data is shared. The audit engine, Judge Audit, is open source and can run in your environment.

Security

To report a vulnerability in our open-source projects, follow the security policy.
